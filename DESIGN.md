# Design System: Edu Platform Course Experience

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Playful, tactile, and consistent with the Profile page. The course experience should feel like soft learning toys on a cheerful desk: rounded, inflated clay surfaces; friendly contrast; and a bright coral/mint/sky/lavender reward palette.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- Profile Coral (#FF6B6B): Primary celebration, CTA warmth, and hero energy.
- Soft Coral Highlight (#FF8E8E): Secondary CTA and friendly hover/gradient partner.
- Mint Reward Green (#4ECDC4): Progress, learning success, and reassuring demo states.
- Sky Progress Blue (#45B7D1): Stats, course discovery, and progress tracking.
- Sunshine Yellow (#FFD93D): XP, badges, reward chips, and playful highlights.
- Soft Sunshine (#FFE066): Inflated yellow clay-card surfaces.
- Lavender Badge (#D4A5FF): Testimonials, language switching, and imaginative sections.
- Deep Slate (#1A2744): Primary readable text on pastel surfaces.

## 3. Typography Rules
Use heavy rounded headings for kid-facing hierarchy, but keep body copy simple and readable. Vietnamese copy must include full accents. Avoid all-caps for long Vietnamese phrases; reserve uppercase for short labels only.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** Pill-shaped, chunky, and candy-gradient. Avoid dark enterprise-style buttons on course pages.
- **Cards/Containers:** Generously rounded clay cards with white borders, pastel fills, and soft downward shadows.
- **Course Media Tiles:** Show course theme or friendly initials only. Never expose technical asset paths such as storage status or `pending:` strings to learners.
- **Progress:** Use visible colorful bars with demo values when live data is empty, so new users still understand the product promise.

## 5. Layout Principles
The first viewport must show the product promise and at least one course preview. Keep progress, catalog, testimonials, and CTA visible as distinct landing-page sections, not only as dashboard statistics.
